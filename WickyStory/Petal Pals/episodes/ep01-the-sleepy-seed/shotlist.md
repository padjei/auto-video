## Continuity that must hold

- **Wish-Bloom**: stage 1 (shot 4) → stage 2 (shot 59). Never both in one frame.
- **Sprout's leaves**: ONE leaf shots 1–59, TWO leaves shots 60–63. This is the single easiest
  continuity error to make and the one most visible to a returning child viewer.
- **Sun position**: morning (low, warm) through shot 43; midday (higher) from shot 44 on.
- **Drizzle** appears only distant/small in shots 62–63. His full design isn't revealed until Ep 2.
