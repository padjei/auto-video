# PETAL PALS — EP 1: "THE SLEEPY SEED"

**Target runtime: 5:00 (300s)** · expanded from the 83s bible outline

**Logline:** A newly planted seed just won't sprout — and the Pals learn that some good things
need patience, gentleness, and a little song.

- **Lesson:** patience & being gentle *(secondary: it's okay to feel discouraged — try again)*
- **Wish-Bloom:** stage 1 (closed bud) → stage 2 (first leaf)
- **Sprout's leaves:** 1 → 2
- **Cliffhanger:** a sad little grey cloud drifts over the sunny garden
- **New elements:** Petunia (baby flower)

---

## ACT ONE — MORNING IN SUNNYDEW (0:00–1:05)

**EXT. SUNNYDEW GARDEN — SUNRISE**

Golden sunrise over candy-colored flower-hills. Dewdrops sparkle and *plink*. Petals open one by
one, like the garden is waking up and stretching. At the garden's heart, the WISH-BLOOM sits as a
small closed bud, dozing.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> In Sunnydew Garden, every sunny morning brings a brand-new little wonder to grow.

A bumblebee hum drifts across the hills. A snail-trail glistens on a petal-path. Somewhere, a
tiny sneeze from a dandelion puffs seeds into the air.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> And today... the garden had a very sleepy secret.

**EXT. TOADSTOOL COTTAGE — MORNING**

The red-capped toadstool house. A little round window pops open. POPPY bursts out, stretching,
wings buzzing.

> **POPPY**
> Good morning, Sunnydew! Petal Pals — there's kindness to grow today!

SUNNY tumbles out of the doorway mid-hum, still half-asleep, and bonks gently into a mushroom.

> **SUNNY**
> *(dreamy)* ...five more minutes, little flower...
> *(waking up, delighted)* Oh! Good morning, Poppy!

SPROUT rockets out past both of them, bouncing.

> **SPROUT**
> Is it today? Is it today?! Is the seed going to sprout TODAY?

DEW emerges last. Very, very slowly. Everyone waits. He is roughly one snail-length out the door.

> **DEW**
> *(serenely)* ...good... morning...

> **POPPY**
> *(warmly)* Good morning, Dew! Right on time.

**[THEME RIDE — full call-and-response]**

> **POPPY (sing):** The sun is up, the dew is bright — Sunnydew is shining light!
> **SUNNY (sing):** We help our friends and watch them grow — Petal Pals, let's go, go, go!
> **SPROUT (sing):** A little kindness, a little care — makes something wonderful everywhere!
> **ALL (sing):** Petal Pals — where kindness grows!

---

## ACT TWO — THE SLEEPY SEED (1:05–2:05)

**EXT. FLOWERBED — MORNING**

A little patch of bare soil with a tiny stick marking the spot. SPROUT and DEW peer at it. Nothing.

> **SPROUT**
> This little seed was planted days ago... but it hasn't sprouted at all. Is it... broken?

> **DEW**
> *(slow, warm)* Not broken, little Sprout. Some good things simply take time... and a little care.

SPROUT leans very close to the soil and cups his hands around his mouth.

> **SPROUT**
> *(loud whisper)* HELLO IN THERE! Are you awake?

Nothing. A beat. He knocks politely on the dirt.

> **SPROUT**
> ...I think it's still asleep.

> **SUNNY**
> *(gently)* Maybe it's having a lovely dream.

> **POPPY**
> Then let's give it our very best care! Petal Pals — gentle and patient!

---

## ACT THREE — TRYING TOO HARD (2:05–2:55)

**EXT. FLOWERBED — CONTINUOUS**

SPROUT's eyes go wide with an idea. He grabs the tiny stick and starts digging enthusiastically.

> **SPROUT**
> I'll help it! I'll dig it up and see what's WRONG with it!

> **DEW**
> *(gently, no scolding)* Little Sprout... wait.

SPROUT freezes, stick in the air.

> **DEW**
> If we pull a seed up to see if it's growing... it cannot grow.

> **SPROUT**
> *(small)* ...oh.

He pats the soil back down. Carefully. Then pats it again, extra gently, and smooths it flat.

> **SPROUT**
> Sorry, seed.

> **POPPY**
> That's the kindest thing you could have done, Sprout.

> **SUNNY**
> Being gentle IS helping.

SPROUT sits down heavily, chin in his hands. His one leaf droops.

> **SPROUT**
> But what if it never wakes up? What if we wait and wait... and nothing happens?

SUNNY sits beside him and leans their shoulders together.

> **SUNNY**
> Then we'll wait together. Waiting is much nicer with a friend.

---

## ACT FOUR — THE CARE SONG (2:55–3:55)

**EXT. FLOWERBED — CONTINUOUS**

POPPY claps her hands. The Pals get to work — a warm, busy little montage.

SUNNY buzzes up and folds back a big leaf, letting a shaft of golden sunshine fall onto the soil.
POPPY carries a sparkling DEW-DROP in both arms and tips it gently. DEW polishes a small stone and
sets it beside the patch, "so it doesn't feel lonely." SPROUT leans close and sings very softly.

> **POPPY (SINGING)**
> A drop of dew, a ray of sun —
> **SUNNY (SINGING)**
> A gentle song for everyone —
> **SPROUT (SINGING)**
> Be patient, friend, and you will see —
> **POPPY (SINGING)**
> How wonderful a seed can be!

**EXT. FLOWERBED — LATER**

The light shifts warmer. The Pals sit in a little half-circle around the soil patch. Waiting.
Sunny hums. Dew's eye-stalks slowly droop, then perk up again.

> **POPPY (TO CAMERA)**
> Growing takes patience — and patience is easier when we do it together. Can you help us wait?
> Let's take one big, slow breath...

*A held beat. Poppy breathes in, and out. The whole garden gently swells and settles with her.*

> **POPPY (TO CAMERA)**
> ...and one more. Ready? In... and out. Ohhh, that was a good one!

The soil shifts. Just a crumb. SPROUT gasps.

---

## ACT FIVE — PETUNIA (3:55–4:35)

**EXT. FLOWERBED — CONTINUOUS**

Slowly, a tiny green shoot pushes up through the soil. It wobbles. It stops. It is very shy.

> **SPROUT**
> *(barely breathing)* ...it's coming...

The shoot hesitates, half-curled.

> **SUNNY**
> *(softly)* It's alright. Take all the time you need.

The Pals hum the care song, very quietly. And the little shoot unfurls — into an adorable, giggly
baby flower. PETUNIA. She blinks in the sunshine.

> **PETUNIA**
> *(tiny, sleepy-happy)* ...good morning! Hehe — I grew!

Everyone gasps with joy. SPROUT does a full backflip. DEW raises one eye-stalk, which for Dew is
an enormous celebration.

> **PETUNIA**
> Was I asleep a long time?

> **SPROUT**
> A REALLY long time! But that's okay. You just had to be patient — and gentle!

> **POPPY**
> Welcome to Sunnydew, Petunia.

---

## ACT SIX — THE WISH-BLOOM (4:35–5:00... and the cloud)

**EXT. WISH-BLOOM — DAY**

The giant closed bud at the garden's heart. It shivers happily — and unfurls its very first
little leaf. Golden sparkles drift.

SPROUT feels something on his head. He goes cross-eyed trying to look up. A brand-new curly leaf
has sprouted next to his first one. He now has TWO.

> **SPROUT**
> *(delighted)* I grew too!

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> And the Wish-Bloom grew — just a little. Because kindness always helps things bloom.

**EXT. SUNNYDEW GARDEN — DAY**

Wide and sunny and perfect. Then — a small grey shadow slides across the flower-hills.

The Pals look up. A small grey cloud drifts across the bright blue sky, all alone. A tiny
sniffle. A single raindrop falls... and plops onto Poppy's nose.

> **POPPY**
> A little grey cloud? On such a sunny day? And... is it crying?

The cloud drifts away over the garden wall. The Pals watch it go.

> **POPPY (TO CAMERA)**
> Who's the little cloud over Sunnydew? Find out next time on Petal Pals! Tap the flower so you
> don't miss it — bye-bye, friends!

She waves. Petunia waves with her whole body. Dew begins to wave and is still waving as we cut.

**TITLE CARD: NEXT EPISODE — "THE GREY LITTLE CLOUD"**

## What I added, and why

The original 83s outline had the shape of the episode but not its heart. Everything below is new;
none of your dialogue was cut.

1. **A cold-open of the garden waking** — establishes the world before anything is asked of the
   viewer, and gives us the "before" state of the Wish-Bloom for the end-of-episode payoff.
2. **Character-introducing entrances** — Sunny sleepy, Sprout impatient, Dew slow. Ep 1 has to
   teach four personalities to a three-year-old before it can tell a story. Dew's slowness
   becomes a runner that pays off in the final wave.
3. **Act Three, "trying too hard"** — Sprout tries to dig the seed up to help it. This is the
   most valuable addition: it dramatises *gentleness* instead of stating it, and Dew corrects
   him without scolding. It also gives the episode a genuine turn.
4. **Sprout's discouragement beat** — "what if it never wakes up?" Patience isn't interesting
   until waiting is hard. Sunny's "waiting is much nicer with a friend" is the emotional core.
5. **A to-camera breathing beat** — direct participation, which this age group loves, and it
   makes the audience *perform* patience rather than watch it. Also a natural place for the
   at-home caregiver to join in.
6. **Petunia's shyness** — the sprout hesitates and needs encouragement, so the Pals' kindness
   is what completes the growth, rather than time alone.
7. **Sprout noticing his own second leaf** — makes the season-long visual tracker something a
   child registers, instead of a detail only an adult would spot.
