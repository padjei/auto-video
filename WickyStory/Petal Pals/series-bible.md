# PETAL PALS — Series Bible

A WickyStory original · sunny daytime kids cartoon · **"Where kindness grows!"**

The bright, warm sister-show to *Nightlight Squad* — sunshine instead of moonlight, a garden
instead of a night town, a season-long goal instead of a mystery. No ghosts, nothing spooky:
gentle problems, kindness, and things that grow.

> **Production note.** The source bible was written for LTX Studio's script → storyboard →
> Elements → timeline flow. Per direction on 2026-08-09 we are **not** using LTX Studio; the
> series is produced in-house. See `production-plan.md` for how each LTX concept maps onto our
> pipeline. The creative content below is unchanged.

## The big idea

In Sunnydew Garden, three tiny best friends — the Petal Pals — help a garden friend with a
little problem every day, using kindness, patience and teamwork. Each good deed helps the
garden's magical **Wish-Bloom** grow one stage closer to blossoming. Over the season, a lonely
little rain-cloud named **Drizzle** learns that the very thing that makes him "different" — his
rain — is exactly what the garden needs to bloom.

- **Audience:** ages 2–6
- **Tone:** sunny, gentle, cozy, encouraging
- **Micro-lessons:** patience, sharing, trying again, being gentle, asking for help, kindness to
  someone different

### The engine (every episode)

sunny morning → a garden friend has a small problem → theme song → the Pals help with a gentle
lesson → the Wish-Bloom grows a stage (and Sprout grows a new leaf — a visual progress tracker)
→ a cliffhanger hint → Poppy's to-camera sign-off.

### The season arc

Grow the Wish-Bloom while befriending Drizzle — introduced as a "problem," revealed as the hero.
Finale: a drought threatens the garden, and Drizzle's happy rain saves the day and makes the
Wish-Bloom finally blossom.

### Cliffhanger engine

Each episode's last 45s opens the next thread (a grey cloud, a dry patch, a mysterious seed),
ends on a titled **NEXT EPISODE** card, and gives an in-character subscribe nudge
("tap the flower!").

## Characters

| Name | Role | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| **Poppy** | leader | bright, warm, confident young girl |
| **Sunny** | the heart | warm, gentle, cheerful, a touch dopey-sweet |
| **Sprout** | the curious one (grows across the season) | young, quick, eager, curious |
| **Dew** | the calm, wise one | slow, soft, kindly — a tiny calm sage |
| **Drizzle** | season arc (lonely cloud → beloved friend) | soft, shy, a little gloomy — warming over the season |
| **Narrator** | storyteller (voice only) | warm, gentle |

**POPPY** — a brave, cheerful little ladybug-girl: rounded bright-red shell with soft black polka
dots, tiny translucent wings, huge sparkly dark eyes, rosy cheeks, a happy confident smile, and a
little green leaf-cap perched on her head.

**SUNNY** — a roly-poly fuzzy bumblebee: soft golden-yellow with fuzzy black stripes, tiny round
translucent wings, big kind eyes, chubby cheeks, a gentle happy smile; always humming.

**SPROUT** — a tiny seedling-child: a round soft-green sprout body, big curious eyes, rosy cheeks,
tiny arms and legs, and ONE curly green leaf on top. *Add a new leaf each episode as a visual arc
tracker — by the finale he has five.*

**DEW** — a small, gentle snail with a glistening pastel-rainbow-sheen shell, soft round eyes on
little stalks, a serene smile; moves slowly and steadily.

**DRIZZLE** — a small, shy grey rain-cloud sprite with a soft droopy face, big watery gentle eyes,
little puffy edges, and a single raindrop always about to fall. Sad-sweet, never scary; he
brightens (fluffier and lighter) as the season goes on.

### Guest characters

- **PETUNIA** (Ep 1) — a giggly baby flower. Tiny, sleepy-happy voice.
- **GOLDIE** (Ep 3) — a tall, proud sunflower. Grand voice, then humble.
- **TWIGGY** (Ep 4) — a tiny wilting sapling. Small, weak voice.

## Locations

- **SUNNYDEW GARDEN** — rolling flower-hills in candy colors, winding petal-paths, a big blue sky
  with fluffy clouds, everything sun-dappled and dewy.
- **THE DEW-POND** — a sparkling little pond ringed with reeds and lily pads.
- **TOADSTOOL COTTAGE** — the Pals' cozy red-capped toadstool house HQ.

## Objects

- **THE WISH-BLOOM** — a giant magical flower bud at the garden's heart. Grows one stage each
  episode: bud → leaves → color → half-open → full blossom.
- **DEW-DROP** — a big sparkling drop of water the Pals carry and share.
- **WATERING-LEAF** — a curled leaf used like a little watering can.

## Songs, voices & packaging

- **Voices** stay identical across episodes — one locked voice per character, reused.
- **Songs**: reuse the theme every episode; each help song is a short call-and-response, one
  voice per shot for clean sync. One instrumental bed reused across the season keeps a
  consistent sound.
- **Visual arc trackers**: the Wish-Bloom grows a stage each episode; Sprout gains a leaf. Both
  give little viewers a reason to watch in order.
- **Packaging**: number every episode, keep them in a *Petal Pals — Season 1* playlist,
  thumbnail = a Pal + the guest in the sunny garden with a bold "EP #", mark **Made for Kids
  (COPPA)**, end each on its titled NEXT-EPISODE card.
