# NISTA Technologies — Launch Film

## Objective
A 60-second cinematic launch film that establishes NISTA Technologies as a credible,
engineering-led software company for mission-critical government work, and drives
federal technology leaders to make contact.

## Audience
Federal CIOs, CTOs, and technology program leaders. Secondary: state/local agency
technology leadership, system integrators, and prime contractors.

## Platform
LinkedIn (feed, sound-off first) + nistatech.com hero placement.

## Duration
60 seconds (1800 frames @ 30fps).

## Aspect Ratio
16:9 — 1920x1080.

## Tone
Sophisticated, credible, mission-oriented. Calm authority. Restraint over spectacle.

## CTA
Modernize with confidence.

## Grounding (source: nistatech.com, fetched 2026-08-08)
Verified facts available for on-screen or narrated use:
- Positioning: "Building Intelligent Software for Mission-Critical Organizations"
- Principle: "We don't just describe technology. We build it."
- Engineering-led delivery: "Every engagement is designed, architected, and delivered
  by engineers — not staffed by intermediaries."
- Mission-critical thinking: "Availability, integrity, and recovery are treated as
  first-class requirements, not afterthoughts."
- Secure-by-design: zero-trust posture, hardened defaults, secure SDLC.
- Seven government disciplines: Artificial Intelligence, Data Engineering, Enterprise
  Software, Platform Engineering, Cloud Engineering, Cybersecurity, Digital Transformation.
- Registrations: SAM.gov active; UEI YGRB3SMAWE6; CAGE 22PQ4; Certified Small Business.
- NAICS: 541511, 541512, 541519, 518210, 541715.
- Serves federal, state, and local agencies, system integrators, and prime contractors.

## Claim guardrails (do not violate)
- NO FedRAMP / ATO / IL-level / DoD authorization claims — none are held.
- NO named agency customers, past performance, or contract awards — none are published.
- NO contract vehicle names (no GSA MAS, CIO-SP, SEWP, OASIS) — none are published.
- SOC 2 and NIST alignment may only be referenced as *readiness / in progress*, never
  as achieved certification.
- NO invented metrics, uptime figures, headcount, or customer counts.
- RhemaPresenter is a real shipped product but is a worship-operations platform; it is
  evidence of shipping capability, not of federal past performance. Keep it out of this film.
